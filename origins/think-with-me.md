- License: MIT

- Conceptual inspiration: [ayghri/i-have-adhd](https://github.com/ayghri/i-have-adhd),
  particularly readable communication, concrete actions, and reduced tangents.

No upstream text or code was copied. This skill develops a distinct guided
discovery workflow: clarify intent, explore consequential choices, and proceed
when enough is known. It does not assume a diagnosis, impose session-wide
formatting, or require explicit invocation before offering guidance.

The workflow uses generalized, fictional scenarios for review. No private chat
transcripts, employer information, or customer details are included.

## Flagship-model revision (2026-09-12)

Kept the guided-exploration workflow and implicit invocation. Shortened discovery text and clarified that fictional/third-party scenarios must not inherit the real user’s profile and that inferred causes/benefits remain hypotheses. Matched tests cover unsettled goals and clear execution requests. No broad rewrite or new workflow dependency.
