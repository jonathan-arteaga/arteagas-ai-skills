# Design DNA

Use this as a taste profile, not a recipe. Preserve the shared craftsmanship while letting each product develop its own identity.

## Shared craftsmanship

- Build calm, legible hierarchy through spacing, alignment, scale, grouping, and typography before adding decoration.
- Use balanced working density: airy for marketing, onboarding, and emotional moments; compact enough for expert and data-heavy work.
- Prefer a few purposeful layers over a flat undifferentiated canvas or a page made entirely of cards.
- Use fine borders, restrained shadows, and modest radii. Reserve full pills for compact tags, statuses, and controls.
- Treat light and dark modes as equally intentional designs.
- Sweat small details: optical alignment, consistent icon weight, readable contrast, responsive behavior, focus states, and polished loading, empty, error, and success states.

## Color and typography tendencies

- Favor softened white, cream, stone, mist, or pale sage backgrounds over stark white when appropriate.
- Favor ink, charcoal, or dark navy over absolute black.
- Use pastel or softened accents selectively for status, selection, labels, atmosphere, and emphasis.
- Allow one larger atmospheric color moment when it gives the product identity; avoid saturating every surface.
- Prefer refined modern sans-serif typography with warmth and personality. Avoid defaulting to serif type or routinely oversized headlines.
- Keep the palette flexible. Color can lead when the product calls for it, provided it remains controlled and purposeful.

## Surfaces, icons, and motion

- Default to layered focus: a tinted foundation, an elevated primary workspace, and only the secondary planes needed for context.
- Avoid card sprawl, heavy shadows, excessive glass effects, and nested containers.
- Prefer precise, thin, monochrome outline icons. Put color in selection, status, and feedback more often than in the icons themselves.
- Make motion noticeable but graceful. Use smooth transitions, restrained depth, and selective delight rather than constant animation.
- Scale feedback with importance: routine actions should whisper; meaningful milestones may briefly glow.

## Software and SaaS mode

- Let the product become the imagery. Show readable, art-directed feature scenes with realistic data, controls, states, and fine component detail.
- Aim for Attio-level product fidelity: isolate and enlarge useful workflows instead of pasting a tiny full-screen screenshot or inventing a generic dashboard collage.
- Keep marketing more expressive and the working product calmer. Use delight selectively in onboarding, empty states, success moments, visualization, and transitions.
- For capable products, favor a compact global navigation rail plus contextual navigation when that improves orientation. Do not force this pattern onto shallow products.
- Use direct, understated language by default. Explain value clearly without hype; vary the voice when the product needs more warmth or personality.
- Prefer calm inline recovery for routine errors, a clear primary action in empty states, and interruption only for truly blocking, destructive, or security-sensitive situations.

## Consumer mode

- Keep the same clarity, restraint, spacing, and craft.
- Allow richer photography, material texture, warmer color, lifestyle emotion, and more sensorial composition.
- Let the physical product and human context lead instead of making consumer work resemble a SaaS dashboard.

## Identity variables

Choose these from the product rather than from habit:

- typeface and typographic rhythm
- palette and accent behavior
- composition and grid
- imagery, photography, illustration, or product UI treatment
- density and information rhythm
- shape and radius language
- motion metaphor and interaction personality
- navigation model
- brand voice and content tone

Vary at least three major identity variables between unrelated projects.

## Creative thesis

Write one sentence that joins the product's purpose with a visual idea. It should be specific enough to guide tradeoffs and distinctive enough that it would sound wrong for another product.

Examples of structure, not reusable answers:

- “Make complex coordination feel like a quiet control room.”
- “Treat recovery as a warm daily ritual, not a performance dashboard.”
- “Turn research depth into an editorial field of evidence.”

Build the design around that thesis, then choose one memorable creative moment. Keep the rest composed.

## Anti-sameness checks

Reject or revise a direction when:

- it remains plausible after swapping the logo with an unrelated company
- it repeats the same font, palette, hero composition, card treatment, and motion pattern used recently
- “minimal” has become empty, generic, or under-informative
- color, gradients, pills, glass, shadows, or animation are present without a job
- the product UI shown in marketing is fake, unreadable, or too vague to prove the product
- every section tries to be the memorable moment

## Inspiration map

Use these references for signals, never for copying. Inspect the current source when a project depends on it because websites change.

- [Cursor](https://cursor.com/home): holistic brand and product craft, restrained palette, precise UI, and confident simplicity.
- [Attio](https://attio.com/): high-fidelity product UI as brand imagery, readable feature scenes, and functional color.
- Apple: hierarchy, material restraint, polish, and graceful motion.
- ChatGPT and Codex: calm functional surfaces, understated controls, and focused interaction.
- X and Grok: dark, direct, product-led expression.
- [Matta](https://matta.ai/): softened paper-like backgrounds, ink-like text, typography, minimalism, and creative scrolling.
- [Snorkel AI](https://snorkel.ai/): subtle color use, dark navy, and clean technical presentation.
- [Vibe3D](https://vibe3d.ai/): typography, color, and creative composition.
- [Eleken](https://www.eleken.co/): clean spacing, typography, and controlled color.
- [Nominal](https://nominal.so/): restrained pastel accents, labels, and pills.
- [Michael Tsirakis](https://michaeltsirakis.com/): clean creative composition; borrow the inventiveness while using less color by default.
- [Oura](https://ouraring.com/) and [Noovo](https://www.noovolife.com/): consumer mode with clean foundations, richer photography, materials, warmth, and color.
