# Review page composition

Use this explicit audit for product-specific page composition and fidelity to the brief. Neutral hierarchy, spacing, type, color, and adaptability reviews belong to `visual-fundamentals-review`.

Read the brief, current design guidance, source, and available rendered evidence. Assess whether section order, emphasis, imagery, proof, and primary actions communicate the product's actual story. A familiar layout, italic heading, compact table, or single font is not evidence of a defect or AI authorship.

Use the anti-pattern references as diagnostic prompts, not universal prohibitions. Keep established identity and accessible, readable design choices. Catalogue diversification applies to new creative exploration, not a demand to change consistent pages in an existing product.

Return findings with location, evidence, user impact, and smallest correction. HIGH blocks or materially misleads; MEDIUM weakens comprehension or product fit; LOW is isolated polish. State inspected pages/states and verification gaps. Do not edit or redesign during an audit.

An inaccurate generated stamp is a documentation inconsistency, not automatically a user-blocking visual defect. Missing stamps are not defects. For system-wide token/component drift, hand off to `design-system-consolidator`; `design-md` alone owns document updates. Deduplicate findings with any supporting review.
